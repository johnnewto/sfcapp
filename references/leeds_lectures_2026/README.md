# Leeds Lectures 2026

Reference R scripts from Marco Veronese Passarella's NARTI training seminar
*"Modelling Monetary Economies of Production: Integrating SFC, IO and AB Approaches"*
(University of Leeds, 9–10 July 2026).

Upstream repository: [marcoverpas/Leeds_lectures_2026](https://github.com/marcoverpas/Leeds_lectures_2026).

## Contents

- `ABM_SIM.R` — agent-based microfoundation of Godley & Lavoie Model SIM (job lottery + FCFS goods market, Monte Carlo).
